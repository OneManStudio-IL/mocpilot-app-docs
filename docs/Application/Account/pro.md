# MocPilot Pro and purchases

MOCPilot is free to use: both editors, all hubs, the Basic theme and 2 hub slots per profile. **MocPilot Pro** unlocks everything else, or you can buy individual items.

## What's free

- Logic Blocks and Quick Links, and all supported hubs, motors and sensors;
- the **Basic** theme with all its controls;
- 2 hub slots per profile;
- sync, the catalog, publishing and the web editor.

## MocPilot Pro

"Everything in MOCPilot, on all your devices":

- **Everything for free**: nothing to buy separately. All paid items are included: themes, special controls and extensions, including all hub slots;
- **AI Assistant**: describe your model and it sets up the hubs, the dashboard and the program, in the app and in the web editor (see [AI builder](/docs/Application/Profiles/AiBuilder));
- **Remote control**: run a profile on another phone, tablet or computer and control it from your device or from the web editor (see [Remote control](/docs/Application/Profiles/RemoteControl));
- **More with time**: new Pro features are added as they're released.

### Plans

| Plan | Price |
|---|---|
| Monthly | $4.99 per month |
| Yearly ("Best value") | $41.99 per year |

Prices may vary by country and store. New subscribers get a **7-day free trial**.

MocPilot Pro is linked to your MOCPilot account, so it works on all your devices; you need to be signed in to subscribe. You can't subscribe in the browser: subscribe in the app on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android or Windows, and Pro works in the web editor too.

To open the Pro page: **Account → Purchases → MocPilot Pro**, or the **MocPilot Pro** card in the tablet side bar.

### Manage or cancel

**Manage subscription** on the Pro page opens the store you subscribed in (App Store, Google Play or Microsoft Store). If you subscribed on another platform, manage the subscription there. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

The page shows the subscription's state: active and when it renews, active until a date with auto-renewal off, when the free trial ends, or a payment problem to fix in the store.

Without internet, Pro keeps working for a while; after that the app shows "MocPilot Pro needs an online check" until it's back online.

## Purchases {#purchases}

Items you buy separately are yours forever, even without Pro:

| Item | What you get |
|---|---|
| A paid theme | all controls of that theme |
| A special control: steering wheel, pedals, gearbox, handbrake, turn signals, train controller | that control in all its styles |
| Additional hub slot (up to 3) | one more hub slot in every profile |

Buy themes and controls in the dashboard's **Add Controller** palette ([how](/docs/Application/Themes/GettingThemes)), and hub slots with **Unlock Slot** in My Hubs.

## Restore purchases {#restore-purchases}

On a new device or after reinstalling the app, sign in with the same account, open **Account → Purchases** and tap **Restore**. The Pro page has **Restore Purchases** too.

On Windows you can also restore Microsoft Store purchases without a MOCPilot account, in guest mode; see [Restore Microsoft Store purchases](/docs/Application/Account/UserAccount#restore-microsoft-store-purchases).

Messages you may see:

- **Subscription linked to another account**: this store account already has MocPilot Pro for another MOCPilot account. Sign in with that account.
- **Nothing to restore**: no active subscription was found for this store account.
- **Almost done**: the purchase went through, but the server couldn't be reached. Pro is activated as soon as the app is online.
