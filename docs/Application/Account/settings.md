# Settings

How to open Settings:

- **Phone**: **Settings** in the bottom bar.
- **Tablet**: **Settings** in the side bar.
- **Computer**: the gear button in the top bar. On a Mac you can also choose **MOCPilot → Settings…** (⌘,); on Windows, press Ctrl+2. Settings opens in its own window.

## Account

On a phone, the top of Settings shows your account, or **Create an account or Sign In** in guest mode. On a tablet the account is at the bottom of the side bar, and on a computer it's the **Account** button in the top bar. See [Account](/docs/Application/Account/UserAccount).

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sound** | app and control sounds (for example, steering wheel and pedal effects) |
| **Vibration** | a short vibration when you touch controls; only on phones that can vibrate |
| **Keep screen on** | keeps the screen from turning off while a profile is open; turn it on for driving. On iPhone, iPad, Android and Windows (not on a Mac) |
| **Language** | the app language; see [Language](#language) below |
| **Dark mode** | dark or light appearance. On first launch it matches your device's appearance |

Remote control isn't in Settings: to let another device control this one, use **Connect a Remote** on the **My profiles** page. See [Remote control](/docs/Application/Profiles/RemoteControl).

## Language {#language}

On first launch, MOCPilot uses your device's language if the app has it, and English otherwise. To change it:

- **Phone and tablet**: tap **Language**, then tap a language. The app switches right away. The list doesn't show the language you already use.
- **Computer**: click **Language**, click a language (it gets a ✓; the one in use says **Current**), then click **Apply** or press Enter. **Cancel** or Esc keeps the current language.

## Accessibility

The app's buttons have names that screen readers (VoiceOver on Apple devices, TalkBack on Android) read aloud. This includes the buttons that show only an icon, such as **Back**, **Close**, **Settings** or **Undo**.

## About

On a phone or tablet, **Version** at the bottom of Settings opens **About MOCPilot** with the Privacy Policy, Terms and Release Notes / News. On a computer, use **Help → About MOCPilot** in the top bar (on a Mac, **MOCPilot → About MOCPilot**).

## Profile settings

Settings that belong to one profile are in the editor:

- **Editor Settings**: block level, launch block by clicking, stop hub ports when program stops; see [Working in the editor](/docs/Application/BlockBuilder/Editor#editor-settings);
- **Dashboard Settings**: background and grid; see [Dashboard](/docs/Application/Dashboard/DashboardOverview#dashboard-settings);
- **Gamepad**: see [Gamepad](/docs/Application/Dashboard/Gamepad).

When you're signed in, app settings are the same on all your devices. See [Sync between devices](/docs/Application/Account/Sync).
