# Account

A MOCPilot account keeps your profiles, settings and purchases safe and available on all your devices.

## Why have an account

- Profiles and settings are stored on the server and synced across your devices.
- You can get everything back if you replace or lose a device.
- Purchases and MocPilot Pro work on all your devices and in the web editor.
- You can publish profiles to the catalog and get notifications.

## Sign in

On the sign-in screen, enter your **Email or Login** and **Password**, or use **Sign In with Google** / **Sign In with Apple ID**.

<Screenshot name="sign-in" alt="The MOCPilot sign-in screen" width={900} />

**Sign in with a QR code** signs you in on this device by scanning the code with your phone and confirming with Face ID or your fingerprint. It needs a passkey on the phone: add one once on mocpilot.com in **Account → Settings → Passkeys**.

## Create an account

Tap **Create an account**: enter your email twice, a username and a password (twice), and confirm your region. Or use **Continue with Google** / **Continue with Apple ID**. Confirm your email with the activation email; some features stay disabled until the account is verified (tap **Resend activation mail** if it didn't arrive).

## Forgot your password

Tap **Reset** next to "Forgot password?", enter your email and type the code from the email to set a new password.

## Guest mode

**Continue without an account** on the sign-in screen opens a short screen that lists what an account gives you. Tap **Continue without an account** there again to start using the app right away. Everything stays on this device only and is lost if you remove the app or lose the device.

You can sign in later: on a phone, tap **Create an account or Sign In** at the top of **Settings**; on a tablet or computer, open **Account** (**Sign In** in guest mode), which shows the same screen with **Create an account** and **Already have an account?**.

### Restore Microsoft Store purchases {#restore-microsoft-store-purchases}

On Windows, the guest screen also has **Restore Microsoft Store purchases**. Microsoft Store purchases belong to the Microsoft account you use in Windows, so you don't need a MOCPilot account to get them back: click the button (the app needs internet), and the app checks the Microsoft Store (**Checking Microsoft Store...**) and unlocks on this PC what that Microsoft account has bought, including a MocPilot Pro subscription.

## Region {#region}

If your account was created before MOCPilot asked for a region, the app asks once on each device, a few seconds after you sign in: **Set your region**. The region helps the app offer the right store and prices. Tap **Choose** to pick your country in **Account details**, or **Not now** to skip. You can change the region at any time in **Account details**.

## Account page

Phone: **Settings → Account**. Tablet: your account at the bottom of the side bar. Computer: the **Account** button in the top bar.

- **Account details**: login, email, region and account ID (tap an item to copy it).
- **Purchases**: see [MocPilot Pro and purchases](/docs/Application/Account/Pro#purchases).
- **Change password**.
- **Sign Out**: see [Signing out](/docs/Application/Account/Sync#signing-out).
- **Delete account**.

## Delete your account

**Delete account** permanently deletes your account data: personal information, preferences, history and the profiles stored on the server. You also lose access to any purchased or subscribed content linked to the account. This can't be undone.

Enter your current password and confirm with **Slide to delete account** (touch screens) or **Delete account** (computer).

An active subscription is managed by the store: cancel it there before you delete the account.

## Notifications

**Notifications** (the bell on a computer; the **Notifications** tab on a phone and tablet) shows the review results of profiles you publish, replies to your support requests and news from the MOCPilot team. You can mark them as read, select them and delete them. Notifications need an account.
