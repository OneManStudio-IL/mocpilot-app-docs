# Catalog and publishing

The **Profile Catalog** is a library of ready-made profiles: official profiles for LEGO® sets made by the MOCPilot team, and community profiles shared by other builders. It's the same catalog as on [mocpilot.com](https://mocpilot.com).

## Open the catalog

- Phone: **+** → **Search Online** on the **My profiles** page.
- Tablet: **Profile Catalog** in the side bar, or **Search Online** in the **New Profile** menu.
- Computer: **File → Search Online**.

The catalog needs an internet connection. If it can't be shown inside the app on your device, tap **Open in browser**.

You can search the catalog by name, description, hashtags, author and set number. Cards show how many times a profile was downloaded.

## Install a profile

Open a profile in the catalog and add it. A copy appears in your profile list.

- As long as you don't change it, the profile runs exactly as published, including paid themes you don't own.
- When the author publishes a new version, the card shows **Update available**. Tap **Update** to install it; nothing changes until you do.
- The first time you change the profile, MOCPilot warns you: after the change, it no longer gets catalog updates, and products you don't own are replaced by the Basic theme or locked. See [Manage profiles](/docs/Application/Profiles/ManageProfiles#paid-themes-in-shared-profiles).

On the website, you can also press **Add to my account** on any catalog profile; it then arrives in the app through [sync](/docs/Application/Account/Sync).

## Publish your profile

Share your model with everyone:

1. Make sure you're signed in and the profile is synced: the catalog copies it from your account and shows you as the author.
2. Open **Edit Profile** and tap **Send to the catalog**.
3. Check the requirements, fill in the form and press **Send for review**.

<Screenshot name="publish-profile" alt="Publish to the catalog page with the list of missing requirements" width={900} />

### Requirements

Before you send it for review, the profile needs:

- a name of at least 3 characters, not a default one like "New profile" or "Untitled";
- a picture;
- a description of at least 20 characters;
- at least one hub;
- something to control it with: a dashboard controller or the gamepad;
- a program with at least one block or row;
- at least one hashtag.

The publish page lists anything that's missing.

### The form

- **Name** and **Description**: what the model does and how to control it.
- **Hashtags**.
- **Visibility**: **Public** (shown in the catalog) or **By link only**.
- **Hubs**: taken from the profile.
- Additional fields, such as the set number, may be requested.
- **What's new**: for a new version.

### Review

A moderator checks every submission. The result arrives in **Notifications**:

- "Your profile … passed review": it's now in the catalog on the website and in the app.
- "Your profile … did not pass review": the moderator's note says what to fix. Fix it and send it again.

The publish page shows the status: waiting for review, published, published with a new version waiting for review, rejected, or hidden.

You can send up to 5 profiles or new versions a month; the page shows how many you have left.

### New versions and unpublishing

Publishing the same profile again sends a **new version** for review. People who installed it see **Update available**. You can also hide a published profile.
