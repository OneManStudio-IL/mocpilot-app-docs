# Frequently asked questions

### Is MOCPilot free?
Yes. Both editors, all hubs, the Basic theme and 2 hub slots per profile are free. Paid themes, special controls (steering wheel, pedals, gearbox and others) and extra hub slots can be bought separately; MocPilot Pro includes all of them plus remote control and the AI builder. See [MocPilot Pro and purchases](/docs/Application/Account/Pro).

### Which hubs are supported?
LEGO® Technic Hub, Technic Move Hub, City Hub, BOOST Move Hub, WeDo 2.0, Robot Inventor, the Powered Up remote, BuWizz 2.0 and 3.0 Pro, SBrick and SBrick Plus, Mould King 4.0 and 6.0. See [Supported devices](/docs/Introducing/SupportedDevices).

### Quick Links or Logic Blocks?
Pick Quick Links for "this control moves that motor": it's ready in minutes, with no code. Pick Logic Blocks for programs with logic: sequences, conditions, variables, sensors. See the [comparison](/docs/Application/Profiles/CreateProfile#choose-the-program-type).

### Can I change the program type of a profile?
No, it's chosen when you create the profile. Create a new profile with the other type.

### I used Controlz. Is Quick Links the same?
Quick Links follows the same idea (rows from a control to a hub) and adds processing blocks, hub sensors as inputs, live values, undo and no row limit. See [Quick Links](/docs/Application/Links/LinksIntroducing).

### Where are the prebuilt profiles for LEGO sets?
In the [Profile Catalog](/docs/Application/Profiles/Catalog) (**Search Online**): official profiles for sets, plus community profiles.

### Can I use several hubs in one model?
Yes, up to 5 per profile: 2 slots are free and 3 more can be unlocked.

### Does it work with a gamepad?
Yes: Xbox and PlayStation style gamepads that your device supports. See [Gamepad](/docs/Application/Dashboard/Gamepad).

### Can I use MOCPilot on a computer?
Yes, on Mac and Windows, and in the browser on mocpilot.com for editing. See [Phone, tablet and desktop](/docs/Introducing/Layouts), [System requirements](/docs/Introducing/SystemRequirements) and [Web editor](/docs/Introducing/WebEditor).

### Can I drive a model from the browser?
The browser can't use Bluetooth hubs. With MocPilot Pro, the MOCPilot app on a phone, tablet or computer next to the model connects the hubs, and the browser becomes the remote. See [Remote control](/docs/Application/Profiles/RemoteControl).

### Does it work without internet?
Yes. Hubs, your profiles and downloaded themes work offline; your changes are uploaded when you're back online. The catalog, the AI builder, remote control and purchases need internet. See [Working offline](/docs/Application/Account/Sync#working-offline).

### Do I need an account?
No, but without one your profiles live on one device only and are lost if you remove the app. An account gives you sync, backup, purchases on all your devices, and publishing.

### How do I share a profile with a friend?
**Share…** creates a `.dat` file; your friend uses **Import Profile…**. Or publish it to the [catalog](/docs/Application/Profiles/Catalog).

### My friend's profile has controls I don't own. What happens?
You can run it as it is. If you edit it, controls of themes you don't own switch to the Basic theme, and special controls are locked until you buy them.

### Can I make my own themes?
Not in the app. Themes are made by the MOCPilot team, but you can configure controls and save your designs. See [Create controls from a theme](/docs/Application/Themes/CreatingControls).

### What should I write in an AI builder request?
Name the hub and say what's connected to each port (drive motor, steering motor, lights, sensor), what each one should do and how you want to control it. Otherwise the AI has to guess the ports and roles, and the result may need fixing. See [How to get a good result](/docs/Application/Profiles/AiBuilder#ai-builder-tips).

### Is MOCPilot made by LEGO?
No. MOCPilot is an independent app. LEGO® is a trademark of the LEGO Group, which does not sponsor or endorse MOCPilot.

### How do I contact support?
In the app, open **Help** and choose **Ask a question** or **Report a bug**. The reply comes by email and in **Notifications**. For the community, join the MOCPilot Telegram group (the link is in **Help**).
